Why Us

Why productions choose us.

Productions call us when:

  • Leadership wants someone who already understands Broadway League contracts, unions, and show calendars, so there's less explaining and more solving
  • Leave and accommodations feel like a legal and operational minefield and no one owns the map
  • Leadership wants fair, impartial investigations and reporting before an issue becomes a grievance, claim, or press story
  • Leaders want a trusted HR perspective on everyday people issues, someone to think a decision through with before it's made
