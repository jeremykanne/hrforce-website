About HR Force NY

Why We Exist

Why HR for Broadway & Live Entertainment

Broadway and touring productions sit at a crossroads of law, contracts, schedules, and human realities. One decision about leave, accommodations, or a complaint can ripple across:

  • Multiple unions and CBAs
  • Federal, state, and city regulations
  • Tight performance schedules and budgets
  • Reputations you can't easily repair

Most shows and live-entertainment organizations don't have a dedicated, theatre-literate HR partner to hold all of that. HR Force NY exists to change that.

We focus on one thing: embedded, trust-first HR for Broadway, touring productions, and live-entertainment organizations.

How We Work

A small, focused practice, not a generic firm.

We're not a training vendor or a generic HR help line. We offer a focused set of services that plug directly into your world:

  • Embedded HR Support
  • Leave & Accommodations Support
  • Investigations & Workplace Concerns
  • HR Advisory for Live Entertainment Orgs

Everything we do is designed to protect both people and the production: fair, defensible processes that keep shows and organizations moving.

Principles

What We Believe

A few principles guide every engagement:

HR shouldn't be improvised under pressure.

The riskiest decisions often happen quickly. You deserve a plan and a partner before that moment.

Fair process protects everyone.

When people know how concerns are handled, trust and compliance both go up.

Context matters.

Advice that ignores your contracts, unions, budget, and schedule isn't useful. We start from your reality, not generic policy.

Calm beats drama.

Sensitive issues don't need more heat. Our job is to lower the temperature, clarify options, and move you forward.

Our Approach

The Kind of Partner We Aim to Be

Clients come to HR Force NY when they need an HR partner who is:

  • Calm and clear in the middle of difficult situations
  • Direct but human, willing to name risk without theatrics
  • Trusted by both leadership and company, not seen as "on one side"

We're here so producers, GMs, company managers, and nonprofit leaders don't have to carry the hardest HR decisions alone.
