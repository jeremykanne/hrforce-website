How We Support Your Production

Embedded HR for Broadway & touring, delivered as clear, practical services.

HR Force NY doesn't sell vague "HR consulting." Every service here is delivered by senior HR leadership, not passed off to a bench of associates. We offer a small set of focused services that plug directly into the life of a show or live-entertainment organization:

  • Ongoing embedded HR leadership
  • Integrated leave and accommodations support
  • Theatre-savvy investigations and complaint handling
  • Advisory for nonprofit theatres and presenters
Best for: Producers, GMs & Company Managers

Embedded HR Retainers

Ongoing, production-literate HR for Broadway & touring productions.

Use this when:

  • Company managers and GMs have quietly become "the HR department"
  • You're tired of gray-zone calls on discipline, conflict, or behavior under pressure
  • You want one HR point person across one or more productions

Best for: Productions with overlapping leaves

Leave & Accommodations Support

Integrated leave and accommodations plans that keep the show running and compliant.

Use this when:

  • You're stuck on how FMLA, ADA, NY paid leave, sick leave, and CBAs interact in a specific case
  • You're worried a misstep could trigger a grievance, claim, or shutdown
  • A key cast or crew member needs leave or accommodations and there's no clear plan

Investigations & Workplace Concerns

Neutral, theatre-savvy investigations for complaints and sensitive conduct issues.
