Investigations & Workplace Concerns

Neutral, theatre-savvy investigations for complaints and sensitive conduct issues.

Talk to Raul

When a complaint lands—about harassment, discrimination, retaliation, bullying, or other conduct—you don't just have a people problem. You have legal, contractual, reputational, and culture risk, all at once.

Most productions don't have anyone who is both skilled at running a fair, impartial investigation and fluent in Broadway League contracts, unions, and the realities of rehearsal rooms, backstage spaces, and tours.

The Investigations & Workplace Concerns service gives your show or organization direct access to Raul [Last Name], a trusted, neutral HR partner, to design and run investigations that protect both people and the production.

Is This for You?

This service is usually the right fit if:

  • You're a producer, GM, or company manager and a complaint has come in that you cannot ignore
  • You're too close to the situation—or perceived as too close—to be seen as neutral
  • You're not confident your internal process will stand up to scrutiny from counsel, unions, regulators, or the press
  • You've handled something informally in the past and now worry it might not have been enough

If you're thinking, "We can't afford to get this wrong, but we also can't stop the show," this is where we come in.

What's Included

A structured, theatre-literate investigation process.

Intake & triage

  • Confidential review of the concern, context, and key players
  • Guidance on immediate steps to protect people and the production while the process is designed

Investigation planning

  • Clear scoping of what will be investigated—and what won't
  • Identification of who needs to be interviewed, in what order, and on what topics

Interviews & fact-finding

  • Trauma-informed, theatre-literate interviews with complainants, respondents, and witnesses
  • Documented notes and evidence gathered in a structured, repeatable way

Findings & recommendations

  • Written findings that separate facts from interpretations
  • Practical recommendations that account for contracts, law, culture, and the operational realities of your show or organization

Process & communication support

Guidance on how to communicate outcomes to the right people (and only the right people). Coordination with show/organizational counsel and, where applicable, unions and the League.

How It Works

Clear phases. Neutral process. Practical outcomes.

Engagement model

Typically project-based, scoped around a specific complaint or cluster of related concerns. Clear phases: intake → plan → investigation → findings → recommendations / follow-through.

Where we sit

Brought in by producers, GMs, or senior leaders as a neutral HR investigator. Works alongside internal HR (if you have it), not instead of it. Coordinates closely with your attorneys; legal strategy remains with counsel.

Integration with other services

For environments facing recurring concerns, investigations work often pairs with an Embedded HR Retainer to catch issues earlier, and/or policy/process design for how complaints are raised and handled going forward.

What You Gain

Productions and organizations using this service typically see:

  • A trusted, transparent process both leadership and company can respect—even when outcomes are difficult
  • Reduced risk of bias or "cover-up" perceptions, because a neutral investigator is involved
  • Clear documentation that supports decisions if questions arise later (unions, regulators, press, or future internal reviews)
  • Less emotional and administrative load on producers, GMs, and company managers who would otherwise be trying to run an investigation on top of running the show

Next Step

If you're already facing a concern—or you sense one building—start before it escalates. Raul will review what's happening, outline options, and help you see what a fair, defensible process could look like for your production or organization.
