Embedded HR Retainers for Broadway & Touring Productions

Ongoing, production-literate HR so your shows aren't improvising high-risk people decisions alone.

Talk to Raul

Broadway and touring productions live in a constant people-risk minefield: overlapping leave laws and CBAs, intense schedules, tight budgets, and reputations on the line.

When a tricky leave request, complaint, or conflict lands on your desk, you shouldn't be workshopping HR between half-hour and curtain.

An Embedded HR Retainer gives your production or office direct access to Raul [Last Name]—a single, trusted HR partner who understands League contracts, theatrical unions, and how shows actually run—so you can make confident people decisions without burning time, trust, or box office.

Is This for You?

This is usually the right move if:

  • You're a lead producer, GM, or company manager on a Broadway or touring production
  • Company managers and GMs have quietly become "the HR department"
  • You're handling leave, accommodations, or complaints often enough that every answer feels risky
  • You've already had at least one situation where you thought, "If this goes wrong, we could be facing a grievance, claim, or headline"

If that sounds like your reality, you don't have an HR "question" problem—you have an HR capacity and judgment problem. The retainer is built for that.

What's Included

Think of this as "your show's HR person," on a fractional basis.

Day-to-day HR advisory

  • Real-time guidance on discipline, performance, conflict, and sensitive conversations
  • Help planning and documenting people decisions so they're fair, consistent, and defensible

Leave & accommodations support

  • Practical answers on how FMLA, ADA, NY Paid Family Leave, sick leave, workers' comp, and CBAs interact for this person on this show
  • Return-to-work and accommodations plans that protect both people and the production

Complaint intake & triage

  • A clear path for cast and crew to raise concerns to a dedicated HR partner
  • Triage to decide when informal resolution is appropriate and when a formal investigation is required

Coordination with counsel, unions, and the League

  • Translation between legal guidance, union obligations, and what's workable for tonight's performance
  • One HR "command center" that connects all the pieces into a single, coherent plan
How It Works

Structure, rhythm, and where we fit.

Structure

Monthly retainer, sized to production or office size and complexity, and phase of the work—pre-production, rehearsal, run, tour, wind-down.

Rhythm

Regular check-ins with your GM and/or company manager. Ad-hoc access for time-sensitive decisions and emerging issues. Clear lines for what's included vs. when a separate project is scoped.

Where We Sit

Engaged by producers and GMs to protect the production by ensuring fair, compliant, trusted processes. Supports company managers and internal HR; doesn't replace them or your attorneys.

What You Gain

With an Embedded HR Retainer in place, productions typically see:

  • Fewer gray-zone decisions made under pressure
  • Lower risk of grievances, claims, and public crises
  • Less HR burden on company managers and GMs
  • More consistent people practices across the life of the production
  • Clearer coordination among League, unions, counsel, and internal teams
