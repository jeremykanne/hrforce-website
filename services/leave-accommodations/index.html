Leave & Accommodations Support

Integrated leave and accommodations decisions that keep the show running and compliant.

Talk to Raul

Broadway and touring productions sit at the intersection of federal, state, city, and contractual obligations. One leave or accommodation request can touch:

  • FMLA
  • ADA
  • NY Paid Family Leave
  • City sick leave
  • Workers' comp
  • Multiple CBAs and riders

Most shows don't have anyone whose job is to hold all of that and still get someone onstage or backstage tonight.

The Leave & Accommodations Support gives your production direct access to Raul [Last Name], an experienced HR partner, to coordinate complex leave and accommodations so you're not guessing your way through the highest-risk calls.

Is This for You?

This service is usually the right fit if:

  • You're a producer, GM, or company manager managing multiple leave or accommodation requests at once
  • You're unsure how all the different laws and CBAs apply in a specific case
  • You're worried that a misstep could turn into a grievance, claim, or public story
  • You've already lost too much time trying to reconcile conflicting advice from different sources

If you're thinking "I know we need to do the right thing—but I'm not sure what that actually is here," this is what Leave & Accommodations Support is built for.

What's Included

Complete leave and accommodations coordination.

Case review & strategy

  • Detailed review of the situation: medical info, role, contracts, jurisdiction, production schedule
  • Clear options mapped out in plain language, with pros/cons for the production and the individual

Integrated leave mapping

  • Alignment across FMLA, ADA, NY Paid Family Leave, sick leave, workers' comp, and CBAs
  • Identification of what's required, what's optional, and where you have judgment and flexibility

Accommodations planning

  • Practical accommodations that work in the real world of rehearsal rooms, backstage spaces, and touring schedules
  • Guidance on documenting offers, denials, and interactive-process conversations

Communication templates & process

  • Language for letters, emails, and talking points so producers, GMs, and company managers aren't writing from scratch
  • Step-by-step outlines for who communicates what, when, and how

Ongoing oversight for complex cases

Monitoring of long-running or sensitive leaves/accommodations, with adjustments as facts, medical information, or production plans change.

How It Works

Two ways to access Leave & Accommodations Support.

As part of an Embedded HR Retainer

Best when you expect ongoing leave/accommodations complexity across a run or a slate of shows. Leave and accommodations become a standing part of your monthly HR rhythm.

As a scoped project or case-based engagement

Best when you have one or a few high-stakes cases and need immediate structure. We scope the work around specific cases, with clear phases and deliverables.

Coordination

In either model, we coordinate with your internal HR (if you have it), show or organizational counsel, and applicable unions and the Broadway League. You keep your legal and medical providers; we turn their input into a coherent, workable plan.

What You Gain

Productions and organizations typically see:

  • Faster, clearer decisions on complex leave and disability questions
  • Reduced risk of grievances, claims, or accusations of unfair treatment
  • Less stress and second-guessing for GMs and company managers
  • More predictable staffing and scheduling because leave plans are thought through, not improvised
  • Documented, defensible processes that hold up to scrutiny—from unions, regulators, and the press

Next Step

If you're facing a current leave or accommodation question—or you see a wave of them coming—start with a conversation. Raul will walk through your current situation, outline options, and help you see whether a focused case engagement or an embedded retainer is the better fit.
