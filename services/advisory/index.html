HR Advisory for Live Entertainment Organizations

Senior-level HR judgment for nonprofit theatres and live-entertainment presenters.

Talk with us
The Gap

Most nonprofit theatres carry Broadway-level HR complexity without a Broadway-level HR partner.

You carry real HR complexity, but you don't have a full-time senior HR leader. Operations, general management, and artistic leadership ends up absorbing every HR decision — on top of everything else.

HR Advisory gives you direct access to experienced, theatre-literate guidance, sized to your staff, budget, and visibility — without adding another FTE.

The gap

  • Contractual obligations on productions
  • Year-round staff under separate employment law
  • Complex leave, accommodation, and conduct cases
  • No senior HR to pressure-test decisions with

Every hard HR call lands on whoever has the most bandwidth — which is usually no one.

Is This for You?

This service is usually the right fit if:

  • You're a managing director, executive director, general manager, or HR lead at a nonprofit theatre, festival, or presenting organization
  • You're responsible for HR decisions but don't have a senior HR partner to pressure-test them with
  • You want consistency between productions and your year-round staff
  • Producers, management, staff, creative teams, cast, and crew all need to trust the process

If you're thinking, "We need someone who understands both our mission and our contracts," this is what advisory is built for.

What's Included

Senior-level HR guidance sized to your organization.

Strategic HR advisory

  • Senior-level HR perspective on decisions affecting staff, artists, and crews
  • Input on structure, roles, and reporting lines as your organization grows or shifts

Policy & process design

  • Review and refinement of HR policies so they work in practice, not just on paper
  • Design of clear processes for leave, accommodations, complaints, and performance issues

Case-based support

  • Guidance on individual leave, accommodation, or workplace concerns when they arise
  • Help aligning legal advice, union obligations, and organizational values into a workable plan

Leadership & board support

  • Briefings and talking points for leadership and boards on HR-sensitive topics
  • Coaching for managers and leaders on handling difficult conversations and change
How It Works

Flexible models sized to your organization.

Engagement model

Typically delivered as either a light-touch retainer (set number of hours per month/quarter), or advisory blocks tied to a specific period or initiative, season planning, restructuring, or a cluster of complex cases.

Where we sit

Acts as a senior HR sounding board for leadership and internal HR. Coordinates with your attorneys and, where applicable, unions and the League. Designed to complement, not replace, your existing operations, HR staff, or legal counsel.

Integration with other services

For organizations that also produce or present, advisory often pairs with the Leave & Accommodations Support for complex cases, and Investigations & Workplace Concerns when formal investigations are required.

What You Gain

Organizations using HR Advisory typically see:

  • More confident HR decisions that account for law, contracts, and culture all at once
  • Stronger, clearer policies and processes that staff, artists, and crews can actually follow
  • Less decision fatigue for leaders who were previously carrying HR questions alone
  • Better alignment between mission, values, and how people decisions are made and communicated
