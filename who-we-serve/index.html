Who We Serve

HR support for the people who carry the risk when something goes wrong—and the responsibility for keeping the show running.

HR Force NY—led personally by Raul [Last Name]—works with leadership teams across Broadway, touring productions, and live-entertainment organizations who need a trusted, theatre-literate HR partner, not generic corporate advice.

Audience

Producers & General Managers

When you're responsible for the production and the reputation.

You're balancing investors, unions, creatives, schedules, and budgets. When an HR issue surfaces—a leave request, a complaint, a conflict—you can't afford to guess your way through overlapping laws and contracts, delay decisions until counsel is free to weigh in on every detail, or hope that an informal fix won't come back as a grievance or headline.

We're a fit if:

  • You want one HR point person across one or more productions
  • You're tired of gray-zone people decisions under pressure
  • You want issues handled in a way that protects both people and the production

Where producers and GMs usually start:

Embedded HR Retainers – Ongoing, production-literate HR leadership → Leave & Accommodations Support – Integrated support for complex leave webs →
Audience

Company Managers

When you're running the show and quietly running HR.

Most company managers are already acting as intake for complaints and concerns, the first stop for leave and accommodations questions, and the person everyone expects to "have the answer" on policies and process. On top of travel, payroll, schedules, budgets, and daily logistics, HR can't be yet another full-time job.

We're a fit if:

  • You're the default HR department for your show
  • You're writing sensitive emails and documentation from scratch
  • You're worried about "getting it wrong" but don't have someone to pressure-test decisions with

Where company managers usually feel the difference:

Embedded HR Retainers – A consistent HR partner beside you, not above you → Leave & Accommodations Support – Clear plans and language for complex cases → Investigations & Workplace Concerns – A neutral investigator when you're too close to the situation →

Nonprofit Theatres & Live-Entertainment Presenters

When you're operating under League/union frameworks without full-time senior HR.

As a nonprofit theatre, festival, or presenting organization, you're often balancing mission, donors, community expectations, and union agreements; asking operations, general management, or HR generalists to carry senior-level HR decisions; and navigating leave, accommodations, restructures, or complaints without a dedicated HR leader.

We're a fit if:

  • You want Broadway-grade HR judgment without another executive headcount
  • You need policies and processes that actually work for your size and structure
  • Board, staff, and artists all need to trust the way people issues are handled

Where nonprofits and presenters usually start:

HR Advisory for Live Entertainment Orgs – Senior-level HR advisory sized to your organization → Leave & Accommodations Support – Support for complex cases that touch multiple laws and contracts → Investigations & Workplace Concerns – Neutral, defensible processes when complaints surface →
